Voice QA Gym

A practice ground where simulated callers phone our Google voice agent, every call gets scored, and the agent is coached to do better.

Open with the one-line idea: this is a gym for our voice bot. Instead of people making test calls by hand, the tool makes hundreds of realistic practice calls, grades every one like a QA supervisor would, and then suggests better instructions for the bot. It only keeps a change if the bot actually scores better on fresh calls, and a person approves before anything goes live.
