Real run, 19 Oct 2026 · demo agent “order_support” in CX Agent Studio · 6 simulated calls

THE PROOF, PART ONE (60 sec). Slow down here; this is the strongest part. We didn’t stop at a mock-up. We built a real voice agent in Google CX Agent Studio, in our own cloud project, and gave it deliberately loose instructions. The first test run found exactly the kind of problems manual testing misses. It completed 5 of 6 calls, but followed our house rules only half the time. It read a customer’s full email address out loud (the call on the right, flagged at turn seven). When a caller asked for a person, it kept offering a callback instead. And only about a third of its replies were short enough to listen to on a phone.
