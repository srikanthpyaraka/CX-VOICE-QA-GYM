Real run, 19 Oct 2026 · demo agent “order_support” in CX Agent Studio · 6 simulated calls

These are real results, not demo data. We created a small order-support voice agent in Google CX Agent Studio, gave it deliberately loose instructions, and ran the six test calls against it. The good news: it completed five of six calls and never made up order details. The problems are exactly the kind people miss in manual testing. It read out a caller’s full email address, which breaks a house rule. It verified identity loosely on an address change. When a caller asked for a person, it kept offering a callback instead of a transfer, which is the one failed call. And most replies were too long to listen to on a phone. On the right is one real call: the caller first gives the wrong order number, then corrects it. The agent handled the correction but read the email back in full, and the scorer flags it at turn seven. Next slide: what one coaching round did to these numbers.
