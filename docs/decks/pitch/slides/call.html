Step 2 · A test call

From a five-line brief to a real phone conversation

The brief

Caller: elderly, speaks slowly

Wants: status of order 51870

Twist: says 51807 first, then corrects

Success: bot uses the corrected number and reads it back

Text mode

Caller and bot exchange typed lines. Takes seconds. Good for checking logic after every change.

Audio mode

The caller’s lines are spoken aloud in a chosen accent and pace, with optional car or street noise, and streamed to the bot like a real call. Every turn is recorded so you can listen back.

A TEST CALL (30 sec). Four or five lines from the business owner become a real conversation. The AI caller improvises and reacts to what the bot says. Text mode runs in seconds, which suits a daily check after every change. Audio mode is the real phone experience: a voice and accent per caller, optional car or street noise, and every turn recorded so you can listen back.
