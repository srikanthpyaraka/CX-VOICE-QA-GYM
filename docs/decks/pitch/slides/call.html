Step 2 · A test call

From a five-line brief to a real phone conversation

The brief

Caller: elderly, speaks slowly

Wants: status of order 51870

Twist: says 51807 first, then corrects

Success: bot uses the corrected number and reads it back

Text mode

Caller and bot exchange typed lines. Takes seconds. Good for checking logic after every change.

Audio mode

The caller’s lines are spoken aloud in a chosen accent and pace, with optional car or street noise, and streamed to the bot like a real call. Every turn is recorded so you can listen back.

Use this real example from the test pack. The left side is everything a business owner writes. The simulated caller turns it into a natural conversation and changes course depending on what the bot says. Text mode runs in seconds. Audio mode is the real thing: Google text-to-speech gives the caller a voice, for example Indian or British English, faster or slower, with background noise mixed in. It is sent to the bot exactly as a phone call would be, and both sides are saved as audio clips you can play in the tool.
