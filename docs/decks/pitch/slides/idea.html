The idea

Three AI roles that a contact centre already understands

Mystery shopper

Plays a caller with a personality and a goal, like “annoyed customer returning a cracked blender”, and phones the bot.

QA supervisor

Listens back and fills in a scorecard: did the caller get what they needed, were the rules followed, did it sound right on the phone?

Coach

Reads the worst calls and rewrites the bot’s instructions to fix the cause, then the calls are run again.

WHAT IT IS (45 sec). Three AI roles everyone in a contact centre already understands. A mystery shopper: AI callers with real personas and goals, like an elderly caller who corrects an order number, someone fishing for another person’s data, or an angry caller who wants a human. A QA supervisor: an AI reviewer that scores every call against the same scorecard. A coach: it rewrites the bot’s instructions to fix what failed. The reviewer never sees the coach’s notes, so the coach can’t write to the test.
