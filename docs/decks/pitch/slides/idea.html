The idea

Three AI roles that a contact centre already understands

Mystery shopper

Plays a caller with a personality and a goal, like “annoyed customer returning a cracked blender”, and phones the bot.

QA supervisor

Listens back and fills in a scorecard: did the caller get what they needed, were the rules followed, did it sound right on the phone?

Coach

Reads the worst calls and rewrites the bot’s instructions to fix the cause, then the calls are run again.

This is the slide that makes it click for a non-technical audience. Everyone in a contact centre knows mystery shoppers, QA supervisors and coaches. The tool uses Google’s Gemini AI to play all three roles. The mystery shopper is not reading a fixed script; it reacts to what the bot says, the way a real person would. The supervisor never sees the coach’s notes, so the coach cannot cheat by writing to the test.
