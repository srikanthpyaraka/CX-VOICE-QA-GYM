How it works

One loop: call, score, coach, re-test

1

Connect

Point it at the Google agent

2

Call

Simulated callers phone the bot

3

Score

Every call graded on a scorecard

4

Coach

AI rewrites the bot’s instructions

5

Re-test

Keep it only if scores go up

6

Approve

A person clicks Apply

Steps 2 to 5 repeat as many rounds as you choose. Each round is a fresh set of calls, so a change has to prove itself, not just look good on paper.

HOW IT RUNS (30 sec). Six steps, left to right. The two that matter most are five and six. A change is kept only if the bot scores better on a fresh set of calls, including exam calls the coach never saw. And nothing reaches the real bot until a person reviews it and clicks Apply.
