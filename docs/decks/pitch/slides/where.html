Where to use it

Five moments in a voice bot’s life

MomentWhat the tool answers
Before go-liveIs it good enough to put in front of customers? A baseline score per call type.
After every changeDid fixing one thing break another? Re-run the full test pack in minutes.
New region or languageDoes it cope with Indian, British or Australian English, fast talkers, noisy lines?
After complaintsTurn a complaint into a test call, then let the coach work on it until it passes.
Choosing a model or vendorSame test pack, two set-ups, one scorecard: a like-for-like comparison.
WHERE IT FITS (30 sec). It’s a quality gate, not a one-off project: before go-live, after every change, in new regions and accents, after complaints, and when comparing models or vendors. One line per audience, if you need it: CX or operations leader: find bot problems before customers do, and prove every change helped. Product owner: your PRD becomes a test suite in minutes, and it shows you which rules the PRD never defined. Engineering: a regression suite for prompts, with before-and-after scores, diffs, run history and a one-command deploy. Risk or compliance: it caught the bot reading out a full email address; every rule is checked on every call, with an audit trail. Sponsor: two weeks, one agent, an existing build, and a clear go or no-go with evidence.
