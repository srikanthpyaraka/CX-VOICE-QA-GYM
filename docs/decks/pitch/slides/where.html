Where to use it

Five moments in a voice bot’s life

MomentWhat the tool answers
Before go-liveIs it good enough to put in front of customers? A baseline score per call type.
After every changeDid fixing one thing break another? Re-run the full test pack in minutes.
New region or languageDoes it cope with Indian, British or Australian English, fast talkers, noisy lines?
After complaintsTurn a complaint into a test call, then let the coach work on it until it passes.
Choosing a model or vendorSame test pack, two set-ups, one scorecard: a like-for-like comparison.
Position it as a quality gate, not a one-off project. Before launch it gives a baseline. After every edit it acts as a safety net, the way software teams run automated tests. When the bot is rolled out to a new country, the audio mode tests accents and line quality. When a customer complains, that call becomes a permanent test. And when someone proposes a different AI model or vendor, you compare them on the same calls and the same scorecard.
