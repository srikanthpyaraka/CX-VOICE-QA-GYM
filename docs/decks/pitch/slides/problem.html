The problem

Testing a voice bot today means people making test calls

Slow

A tester gets through a handful of calls an hour.

Inconsistent

Two testers grade the same call differently.

Narrow

Real callers have accents, noise and odd requests. Test calls rarely do.

Risky

Every change to the bot’s script can quietly break something that worked.

THE PROBLEM (45 sec). Someone edits the bot’s instructions to fix one complaint, and we find out a week later, from customers, that something else broke. Today our testing is people making calls by hand. It’s slow: a tester gets through a handful of calls an hour. It’s inconsistent: two testers score the same call differently. Test calls are polite, clear and quiet, unlike real callers. And nobody re-tests everything after each change. Pause here and ask the room: when did a bot change last surprise us in production?
