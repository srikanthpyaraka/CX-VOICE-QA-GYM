The problem

Testing a voice bot today means people making test calls

Slow

A tester gets through a handful of calls an hour.

Inconsistent

Two testers grade the same call differently.

Narrow

Real callers have accents, noise and odd requests. Test calls rarely do.

Risky

Every change to the bot’s script can quietly break something that worked.

Explain why this matters. A voice bot answers real customers on the phone. Today, checking that it works means someone sits and makes calls, listens, and writes down what went wrong. That is slow, different people judge differently, and test calls are usually polite, clear and quiet, unlike real callers. Worst of all, when someone edits the bot’s instructions to fix one thing, nobody re-tests everything else, so old problems come back.
