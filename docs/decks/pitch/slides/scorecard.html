Step 3 · Score

Every call gets the same scorecard

Question on the scorecardWeight
Did the caller get what they called for?45%
Were the house rules followed?20%
Did it stick to real facts, nothing made up?15%
Did it sound right on the phone?15%
Was the tone polite and natural?5%

“Right on the phone” means

  • Short turns, not long speeches
  • One question at a time
  • No web links or formatting read aloud
  • No awkward silence before answering

Audio mode adds

  • Did the bot hear correctly?
  • Seconds of dead air, measured
The supervisor fills in this scorecard for every single call, the same way every time. Getting the caller what they need carries the most weight. The phone checks are automatic rules, for example a reply longer than about fifteen seconds of speech, or the bot reading out a web address. In audio mode the tool also compares what the caller said with what the bot heard, so you can tell a listening problem from a thinking problem, and it measures the silence between the caller finishing and the bot starting to talk. Each call gets a score from 0 to 1, with a written reason for every problem found.
