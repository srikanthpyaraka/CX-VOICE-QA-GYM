Be straightforward about maturity. The application is built and its logic is tested end to end against simulated agents, but it has not yet been run against one of our real Google voice agents. That first run is the next step. Name the limits plainly: simulated callers cannot barge in while the bot is talking, keypad presses are not simulated yet, audio tests take real time, and the AI scorecard should be checked against a human reviewer on a sample of calls before anyone relies on the numbers.
