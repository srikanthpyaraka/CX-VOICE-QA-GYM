This pop-up is where people build trust in the scores. You can read the whole conversation, press play on any turn to hear exactly what the caller and the bot said, and see the orange flags on each bot turn. When the bot’s speech recognition got something wrong, a line shows what it actually heard and how much was wrong. On the right, the scorer explains its verdict and points to the turn numbers, so anyone can check whether they agree.
